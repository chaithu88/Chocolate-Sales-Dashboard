# 🍫 Chocolate Sales Dashboard

### Power BI | Sales Analytics | Business Intelligence

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze chocolate sales performance across products, salespersons, countries, shipments, and time periods.

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

* **Sales & Profit Overview** — Track total amount, boxes sold, shipments, profit, and profit margin.
* **CY vs PY Analysis** — Compare current-year and previous-year performance.
* **Geographic Analysis** — Analyze sales contribution across different countries.
* **Product Analysis** — Identify top-performing products and compare product-level sales and profitability.
* **Salesperson Analysis** — Compare the performance of the top salespersons based on sales, boxes sold, and profit percentage.
* **Shipment Distribution** — Understand shipment volume and its distribution across orders.
* **Time Analysis** — Explore performance across the selected date range using interactive filters.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

**Power BI · DAX · Power Query · Excel · Data Modeling · Data Visualization**

## 📁 Project Files

* 📊 **Dashboard** — Interactive Power BI `.pbix` report
* 📂 **Dataset** — Source sales data

## 🎯 Objective

To transform raw chocolate sales data into an **interactive business intelligence dashboard** that provides clear insights into sales performance, profitability, product trends, geographic contribution, and salesperson performance.
